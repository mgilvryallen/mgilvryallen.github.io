M'Gilvry Allen's apps

Small apps for playing music, cooking, and keeping track of things. They run in your browser or on your phone's home screen, work offline, and ask for no sign-up.

On your phone: open one, then on an iPhone tap Share and Add to Home Screen; on Android, open the menu and tap Install app. It opens like any other app from then on.

Make your own: every app is free to copy. Its code link has a Use this template button, and the README says how to set it up.
